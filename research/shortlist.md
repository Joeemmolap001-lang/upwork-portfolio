## Recommended four

Chosen for range (four different kinds of client), a paying customer, and a strong live demo:

1. **Invoice chaser (#1)** – B2B, shows payments/email automation, the most common Upwork request type.
2. **Shift cover board (#2)** – hospitality/retail, shows real-time multi-user features and roles.
3. **Event RSVP + headcount (#3)** – consumer, public link anyone can try in 10 seconds; great first demo.
4. **Care circle (#4)** – shared family data, calendar and notifications; a story clients remember.

Alternates if one of the above doesn't appeal: **Solo-trade back office (#12)** (very sellable, more
integrations), **Custom-order tracker (#13)** (fast build, clear niche), **License tracker (#8)** (simple,
recurring revenue).
